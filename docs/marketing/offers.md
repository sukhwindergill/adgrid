# Offers — AdGrid

## Advertiser-side offers
- **First-campaign credit** (e.g. $25-50 ad credit) — lowers activation risk for a self-serve product with an unfamiliar category (DOOH) for small local advertisers.
- **Launch-city founding advertiser rate** — locked-in discounted rate for the first N advertisers in a new city, creates urgency without fake scarcity (it's real: first N is a real limited cohort).

## Operator-side offers
- **Zero platform fee for first 90 days** — accelerates supply-side liquidity in new cities (directly supports the launch-plan guardrail).
- **Referral bonus** — see `referrals.md`.

## Rules
- Offers must be real and time/quantity-bound (ties to the anti-fake-scarcity note in `marketing-psychology.md`).
- Every offer needs a UTM/ref-coded landing page per `attribution.md` so lift is measurable.
- Prefer operator-side offers over advertiser-side discounts while supply is the binding constraint (per `launch-plan.md`).
