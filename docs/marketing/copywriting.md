# Copywriting Notes — AdGrid

## Change made
`src/views/marketing/sections/Hero.jsx` hero subhead now includes "no minimums" — the single biggest objection-handler for the advertiser side (validated by its repetition in `HowItWorks.jsx`, `SiteSearch.jsx`, and `faqData.js`) was previously absent from the highest-visibility copy on the page.

## Copy principles for AdGrid (apply to future sections/emails/ads)
1. **Lead with the two-sided benefit, resolve in one line who's talking.** Operators and advertisers read the same homepage — every section should make clear within the first sentence which side it's addressing.
2. **Numbers over adjectives.** "8 venue categories," "Toronto & Vancouver" beat "wide selection," "growing fast."
3. **Kill friction words up front.** "Free," "no minimums," "in minutes" — surface these early, not buried in FAQ.
4. **CTA verbs match the action, not generic "Get started."** Already good practice in the repo: "List your screens," "Book a campaign."
