# Marketing Loops — AdGrid

## Loop 1: Operator referral loop
Operator earns a payout → prompted to refer another business owner (same vertical/network) → referred operator signs up → both get a bonus. Compounds supply-side growth, which is the scarcer/higher-leverage side of the marketplace early on.

## Loop 2: In-venue discovery loop
Screen displays AdGrid-branded ad occasionally / QR code → passerby or advertiser notices → visits AdGrid → becomes an advertiser or a new operator (if they also own a venue). This is a physical-world loop unique to OOH and worth deliberately designing into the on-screen creative rotation (small watermark or house-ad slot, ties to the existing house-ads feature).

## Loop 3: Content/SEO loop
Programmatic city pages (`programmatic-seo.md`) rank for local search → drive both operator and advertiser signups → more screens/campaigns per city → richer, more specific city page data → better rankings. Self-reinforcing as long as new cities keep crossing the minimum-liquidity threshold.

## Design principle
Prioritize loops that grow operator supply over loops that only grow advertiser demand — per the launch-plan guardrail, demand without supply liquidity actively hurts trust.
