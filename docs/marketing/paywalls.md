# Paywalls — AdGrid

## Applicability
AdGrid is a transaction/take-rate marketplace (operators list free, advertisers pay per booking/slot), not a subscription SaaS product — there is no traditional content/feature paywall to design. This skill's core content (metered paywalls, hard paywalls, freemium feature gating) does not map directly onto the current business model.

## Where the concept still applies
- **Operator side**: consider whether any advanced features (e.g. detailed analytics, multi-screen bulk management) should be gated behind a volume/tier threshold as the platform matures — not a paywall today, but a future monetization lever worth flagging for `revops.md` and `pricing.md`.
- **Advertiser side**: the "payment required before campaign goes live" step functions as a soft paywall — apply CRO principles from `cro-audit.md` to that checkout step rather than traditional paywall UX patterns.

## Recommendation
No changes made — revisit if/when AdGrid introduces tiered operator plans or premium analytics features.
