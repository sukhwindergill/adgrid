# Marketing Plan — AdGrid (Master Index)

AdGrid is a self-serve marketplace connecting Canadian digital screen operators with local advertisers. This is the index into the full marketing plan produced across the `marketing-skills` pass — see `docs/marketing/` for every linked doc.

## Strategic priorities (in order)
1. **Supply-side liquidity first** — operator acquisition and retention outrank advertiser acquisition until per-city screen density clears the trust threshold (`launch-plan.md`, `ads.md`, `churn-prevention.md`).
2. **Self-serve, no-minimums positioning** — the core wedge vs. traditional OOH and vs. "just boost a Facebook post" (`competitors.md`, `copywriting.md`).
3. **Two launch cities (Toronto, Vancouver) before geographic expansion** — every channel plan (ads, content, events) should key off current live cities, expanding only as `programmatic-seo.md` city pages hit density.

## 90-day focus
Weeks 1-4: operator seeding in both cities (cold-email, community). Weeks 5-8: advertiser acquisition ramp gated by live screen count. Weeks 9-12: first churn/retention read, first case-study content, evaluate expansion city.
