# Competitor Profiling — AdGrid

## Direct competitors (self-serve/programmatic DOOH marketplaces)
- **Broadsign / Vistar Media / Place Exchange** — programmatic DOOH SSPs, but built for agency/DSP buyers, not local self-serve advertisers. AdGrid's wedge: no agency or DSP required, self-serve for a $50 local business.
- **Traditional OOH reps (Pattison, Astral)** — enterprise sales motion, high minimums. AdGrid's wedge: no minimum spend, instant self-serve signup.

## Indirect competitors (where the ad budget actually competes)
- **Meta/Google local ads** — the real default a small business reaches for. AdGrid's positioning must answer "why not just boost a Facebook post" — physical, local, always-on presence vs. feed ads.
- **Static print/signage** — for the operator side, AdGrid competes with "just run a slideshow of stock photos" (i.e., doing nothing) more than with another paid product.
