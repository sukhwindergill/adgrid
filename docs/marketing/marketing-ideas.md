# Marketing Ideas Brainstorm — AdGrid

## High-confidence, low-cost
1. Earnings calculator as a standalone lead-gen tool (`free-tools.md`).
2. Operator referral program: refer another operator, both get a payout bonus (`referrals.md`).
3. "Screen of the week" social feature — highlight a real operator's earnings/placement, tag their business.
4. Weekly automated "you earned $X" email as a retention + shareability hook (screenshot-worthy).

## Medium-confidence, worth testing
5. Local BIA partnership bundles — free trial credit for BIA member businesses.
6. Programmatic city landing pages for SEO long-tail capture (`programmatic-seo.md`).
7. Comparison content vs. "boosting a Facebook post" targeting advertiser search intent.

## Speculative / longer-term
8. AdGrid-branded screen decal/QR code kit for operators — reinforces legitimacy in-venue and drives passerby curiosity/signups.
9. Seasonal local-business campaigns (e.g. "Small Business Week" bundle pricing) tied to `offers.md`.
10. API/embed widget letting operators show live "screen earnings" on their own website — organic backlink + trust signal.

## Explicitly deprioritized
Large sponsored events, broad-audience influencer deals — see `events.md` and `influencer-marketing.md` for reasoning.
