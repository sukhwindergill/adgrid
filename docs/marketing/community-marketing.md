# Community Marketing — AdGrid

## Where the community lives
- Local Facebook groups / subreddits for small business owners in launch cities (r/smallbusiness, city-specific business subreddits).
- Independent gym owner / cafe owner communities (Facebook groups, industry Slack/Discord communities).
- Local BIA (Business Improvement Area) events and meetups.

## Approach
- Participate as a resource, not a pitch: answer "how do I monetize a screen I already have" type questions, disclose affiliation, link only when directly relevant.
- Seed operator success stories ("$X/month from a screen that used to just show a menu") as organic case-study posts once real operator data exists.
- Host a low-key local meetup or booth at a small-business/BIA event in each launch city to recruit operators face-to-face — high-trust channel for a two-sided marketplace's supply side.

## Guardrail
Never post from AdGrid's own account into a community without prior engagement history there — cold self-promotion in local business groups burns trust fast and is exactly the pattern that gets a brand banned.
