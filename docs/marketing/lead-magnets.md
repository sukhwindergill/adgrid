# Lead Magnets — AdGrid

## Operator-side
- **"How much your screen could earn" report** — email-gated version of the calculator in `docs/marketing/free-tools.md`, personalized by venue type/city.
- **"Screen Monetization Checklist"** — short PDF/page: what makes a screen approvable, placement tips, creative-approval basics.

## Advertiser-side
- **"Local DOOH cost guide"** — city-by-city estimated cost ranges, positioned against the comparison table in `docs/marketing/competitors.md`.
- **Free trial / first-campaign credit** — arguably a stronger lead magnet than content for a self-serve product; ties to `docs/marketing/offers.md` and `docs/marketing/popups.md` for exit-intent capture.

## Distribution
Gate lead magnets behind email capture only where the content is substantial (guide/report); keep the calculator itself ungated to maximize top-of-funnel reach and use it as a soft-gate upsell into the full report.
