# Referral Program — AdGrid

## Operator referral (primary — supply-side leverage)
Existing operator refers another business owner → both receive a bonus once the referred operator's screen goes live and earns its first payout (bonus tied to a real earned outcome, not just signup, to avoid low-quality referrals). Trigger point: right after the referring operator's own first payout email (`emails.md`) — highest-goodwill moment per `marketing-psychology.md`.

## Advertiser referral (secondary)
Advertiser refers another local business → both get a discount/credit on their next campaign (ties to `offers.md`).

## Mechanics
- Unique referral code/link per user, tracked via the `ref` param convention in `attribution.md`.
- Dashboard surface showing referral status ("2 invited, 1 signed up, 1 live") — reinforces progress and prompts sharing again.

## Why operator-first
Per `launch-plan.md`, operator supply is the binding constraint on marketplace liquidity — a referral program should be weighted to grow the scarcer side first, with advertiser referrals as a secondary/later addition once city liquidity is healthy.
