# Ad Creative — AdGrid Acquisition Campaigns

Creative direction for AdGrid's own paid acquisition (Meta/Google) targeting the two sides of the marketplace.

## Operator-side (screen owners: gyms, cafes, retail, condos)
- **Headline A**: "Turn your idle screen into rent." / Body: "AdGrid fills your dead airtime with local ads and pays you weekly. List free, set your own price."
- **Headline B**: "Your screen already earns attention. Now make it earn money." / Body: "Real-time pricing, no minimums, cancel anytime."
- **Visual direction**: real screens in real Canadian small businesses (gym, cafe, laundromat) with a subtle earnings overlay ("$340 this month"). Avoid stock photography — use operator-submitted screen photos where available.
- **CTA**: "List your screen free"

## Advertiser-side (local businesses)
- **Headline A**: "Put your ad on screens down the street — today." / Body: "Self-serve local OOH. Pick a screen, set a budget, launch in minutes."
- **Headline B**: "Billboard reach. Corner-store budget." / Body: "No agency, no minimum spend, real-time pricing."
- **Visual direction**: map-pin style creative showing nearby screens (leverages the existing `react-leaflet` screen map in `src/App.jsx`), paired with a mockup of the operator's actual creative on-screen.
- **CTA**: "Find screens near you"

## Format checklist
- Square (1:1) + vertical (9:16) cuts of every concept for Meta/Instagram placements.
- Keep primary text under 125 characters for Meta feed truncation.
- Always A/B the headline against the "free to list / no minimum spend" objection-handling line (see `docs/marketing/ab-testing.md`).
