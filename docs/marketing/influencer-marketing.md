# Influencer Marketing — AdGrid

## Fit assessment
Low-to-medium fit for a B2B-leaning local marketplace, but a specific niche works well: **local small-business content creators** (the "small business owner" TikTok/Instagram niche) who already talk about revenue hacks, side income, and operations for gyms/cafes/retail.

## Approach
- Partner with 3-5 micro-influencers (5k-50k followers) in the small-business-owner niche per launch city, offering them a trackable referral code (ties to `docs/marketing/referrals.md` and `docs/marketing/attribution.md`) rather than a flat fee — aligns incentive with actual signups.
- Content angle: "I turned my [gym/cafe]'s dead screen into a $X/month revenue line" — authentic operator-side story, not scripted ad copy.
- Avoid large generic lifestyle influencers — audience mismatch for a two-sided B2B marketplace.

## Not a priority right now
Paid brand-awareness influencer deals — better spend is direct operator prospecting (`docs/marketing/cold-email-templates.md`) and community participation (`docs/marketing/community-marketing.md`) at AdGrid's current stage (two launch cities, pre-scale).
