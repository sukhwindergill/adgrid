# Launch Plan — AdGrid

## Pre-launch (both sides need minimum liquidity before public push)
- Seed 15-20 operators per launch city (Toronto, Vancouver) via direct outreach (`docs/marketing/cold-email-templates.md`, `docs/marketing/community-marketing.md`) before opening advertiser signup broadly — an empty map kills advertiser trust on day one.
- Verify `llms.txt`, OG tags, sitemap (`public/sitemap.xml`, `public/llms.txt`) are live before any press/directory push.

## Launch day
- Product Hunt launch (coordinate with `docs/marketing/directory-submissions.md`).
- Press outreach to Canadian local-business and ad-tech press (`docs/marketing/public-relations.md`).
- Directory submissions batch-submitted same week.
- Social announcement across owned channels (`docs/marketing/social.md`).

## Post-launch (weeks 1-4)
- Daily monitoring of signup funnel (`docs/marketing/analytics.md`) for both sides — operator listing completion and advertiser checkout completion.
- Weekly digest of new operator earnings/fill-rate as social-proof content fuel (`docs/marketing/content-strategy.md`).
- First churn/retention read at day 30 (`docs/marketing/churn-prevention.md`).

## Guardrail
Do not scale advertiser-side paid acquisition (`docs/marketing/ads.md`) faster than operator-side inventory growth in a given city — protects marketplace liquidity and first-impression trust.
