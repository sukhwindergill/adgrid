# Content Strategy — AdGrid

## Pillars
1. **Operator earnings** — "how to monetize a screen you already have," fill-rate benchmarks, case studies by vertical (gym/cafe/retail/condo).
2. **Local advertiser education** — "what is DOOH," "how much does screen advertising cost," self-serve how-tos.
3. **City/market content** — programmatic city pages (see `docs/marketing/programmatic-seo.md`) supported by local screen-count/market stats.
4. **Product/trust** — pricing transparency, house-ads/fill explainer, privacy/terms.

## Cadence
- 1 operator-side case study or earnings post per month once real operator data exists.
- 1 advertiser-education post per month targeting a specific "how to advertise on [city] screens" long-tail query.
- Programmatic city pages generated/maintained continuously as new cities reach minimum screen density.

## Distribution
Owned (blog once created) → SEO; community channels (`docs/marketing/community-marketing.md`); cold email nurture (`docs/marketing/cold-email-templates.md`); co-marketing partners (`docs/marketing/co-marketing.md`).

## Current gap
Repo has no blog/content directory yet — first step before this pillar strategy can ship content is standing up a `/blog` or `/guides` route in `src/views`.
