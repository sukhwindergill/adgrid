# Prospecting — AdGrid Operator Acquisition

## Ideal Operator Profile (IOP)
- Independent or small-chain local business with foot traffic and dwell time: gyms, cafes, laundromats, barbershops/salons, condo/apartment lobbies, retail waiting areas.
- Already has (or is willing to install) a digital screen — no existing screen is a bigger conversion lift than an idle one.
- Located in a current launch city (Toronto, Vancouver) — outside these, park in a waitlist rather than actively prospect (liquidity guardrail from `launch-plan.md`).

## Sourcing lists
- Google Maps/local business directories filtered by category + city.
- BIA member directories in launch cities (`directory-submissions.md`, `co-marketing.md`).
- Referrals from existing operators (`referrals.md`).

## Qualification signal
Visible screen in storefront photos (Google/Instagram) = high-intent prospect for the cold-email template in `cold-email-templates.md`; no visible screen = lower priority, needs the "you don't have a screen yet" angle which is a harder sell — deprioritize.

## Prospecting cadence
Batch by city + vertical (e.g. "Toronto gyms" this week, "Vancouver cafes" next) so cold-email personalization and case-study content can reuse research within a batch.
