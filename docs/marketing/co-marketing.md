# Co-Marketing — AdGrid

## Candidate partners
- **POS / booking software used by AdGrid's operator base** (gym management platforms, cafe POS systems, condo management software) — co-marketing angle: "turn the screen you already have into revenue."
- **Local business associations / BIAs (Business Improvement Areas)** in launch cities — cross-promote to their member directories.
- **Screen/display hardware resellers** — bundle "buy a screen, monetize it with AdGrid" offers.
- **Local marketing agencies** — refer their small-business clients to AdGrid's self-serve advertiser flow as a low-cost OOH option; agencies get a referral credit (ties to `docs/marketing/referrals.md`).

## Format ideas
- Joint webinar/guide: "5 ways to monetize unused screen space" co-branded with a gym/retail SaaS partner.
- Co-branded landing page per partner with a tracked signup link (`docs/marketing/attribution.md` UTM taxonomy, `ref` param).
- Directory/marketplace cross-listing with complementary local-business tools (see `docs/marketing/directory-submissions.md`).

## Guardrail
Only pursue partners whose audience overlaps with one specific marketplace side (operator or advertiser) — mixed-audience partnerships dilute the CTA and are hard to measure cleanly.
