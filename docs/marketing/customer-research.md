# Customer Research Plan — AdGrid

## Research questions by side

**Operators**
- What made you say yes to listing your screen (or no, if you declined)?
- What's your mental model of "how much will this earn me"?
- What would make you delist / stop trusting the platform?

**Advertisers**
- What did you compare AdGrid to before booking (social ads, print, nothing)?
- What made the booking flow feel trustworthy or untrustworthy?
- What would make you book again / not book again?

## Methods
- Post-signup micro-survey (1 question, e.g. "What almost stopped you from signing up?") — cheap, high response rate.
- 15-minute interviews with the first 10-15 operators and advertisers in each new launch city — direct input into `docs/marketing/content-strategy.md` case studies and `docs/marketing/cro-audit.md` findings.
- Churn exit survey for operators who delist (feeds `docs/marketing/churn-prevention.md`).

## Where findings should land
Update `docs/marketing/customer-research.md` (this file) with a running "insights" log below as research happens, and feed validated objections into copy (`docs/marketing/copywriting.md`) and the experiment backlog (`docs/marketing/ab-testing.md`).
