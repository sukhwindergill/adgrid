# Events — AdGrid

## Event types worth pursuing
- **Local BIA / small-business meetups** in launch cities (Toronto, Vancouver) — direct operator recruitment, high-trust channel (ties to `docs/marketing/community-marketing.md`).
- **Ad-tech / DOOH industry events** — brand awareness with agencies and property owners at scale (lower priority pre-product-market-fit given AdGrid's self-serve, non-agency positioning).
- **Product Hunt launch day** as a virtual "event" — coordinate with `docs/marketing/launch-plan.md` and `docs/marketing/directory-submissions.md`.

## Low-cost format: "screen owner office hours"
A recurring 30-minute virtual drop-in for prospective operators to ask questions live — cheap to run, generates first-hand objections for `docs/marketing/customer-research.md` and content ideas for `docs/marketing/content-strategy.md`.

## Not a fit right now
Large sponsored conference booths — high cost relative to AdGrid's current stage (two launch cities); revisit once operator volume justifies a Canada-wide sales presence.
