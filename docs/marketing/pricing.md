# Pricing Strategy — AdGrid

## Current model (per FAQ/HowItWorks copy)
Real-time, transparent per-slot pricing set by supply/demand; no minimums, no long-term contracts. Operators set/influence their own price; take rate presumably funds the platform (exact rate not found in marketing copy — recommend confirming it's stated clearly somewhere in the advertiser flow for trust).

## Recommendations
1. **Publish the take rate or fee structure explicitly** if not already visible pre-checkout — pricing transparency is AdGrid's core differentiator (`competitors.md`), so hidden fees anywhere would directly undercut the brand promise.
2. **City-level pricing benchmarks** — once volume exists, publish average CPM/per-slot ranges by city/venue type; supports both `content-strategy.md` SEO content and advertiser trust (removes "how much does this actually cost" uncertainty before they even sign up).
3. **Operator earnings transparency** — mirror the same principle: show typical earnings ranges by venue type on the operator-facing side (ties to `free-tools.md` calculator).

## Guardrail
Don't introduce tiered/subscription pricing without revisiting `paywalls.md` — the current take-rate model is core to the "no minimums, free to list" positioning; adding subscription tiers is a significant positioning change, not a small pricing tweak.
